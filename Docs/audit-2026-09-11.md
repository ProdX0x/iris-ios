## Manual review (mission checklist)

- Separation of responsibilities: engine (pure), services (protocols), presentation (ViewModels and views), DI (one container). OK.
- Sendable / MainActor: all Domain and GameEngine types are `Sendable` value types; services and ViewModels are `@MainActor`; `SineSynth` is `@unchecked Sendable` with a documented ownership split (control side under lock, render side audio-thread only). OK.
- ARKit lifecycle: `run` on start, `pause` on background and stop, `run(options: [])` on resume; delegate callbacks on the main queue with `assumeIsolated`; frames never retained. Interruptions mapped to phases. OK.
- AVAudioEngine lifecycle: session `.ambient`, engine rebuilt on configuration change and media reset, interruption began/ended handled, observers owned by a bag released with the service (no retain cycle after the `[weak self]` fix). OK.
- Tasks: only `Task { await viewModel.requestAccess() }` in `CameraAccessView` (view-owned). No detached tasks. OK.
- Strong references / leaks: ViewModels hold services strongly and the navigator weakly; the coordinator holds the ViewModel; `CADisplayLink` uses a weak proxy; audio observers are weak. OK.
- Real-time loop allocations: `SineSynth.render` copies a fixed-size struct through a try-lock and mutates pre-allocated arrays in place. OK.
- deltaTime stability and frame drops: clamp 0.1 s, sub-steps of at most one reference frame, exact fractional factors (ADR-3). OK.
- Navigation determinism: single route enum, single phase enum, transitions as methods, tested. OK.
- Accessibility: labels on buttons, modal overlays, combined HUD elements, hidden canvas, Reduce Motion honoured, 44 pt targets. OK.
- Contrast: primary and secondary text above 4.5:1 on the dark ground; tertiary footnotes at the threshold. OK.
- Permissions: `NSCameraUsageDescription` in French; explanation before the system prompt; denied and restricted states; Settings shortcut. OK.
- Error states: unsupported device, camera denied or restricted, AR failure, interruption, background, audio unavailable, all with a visible overlay or screen. OK.

## Fix plan

Mission checklist (section 44 of the brief):
- X or Y inversion, 180 degree rotation, wrong frame: no longer assumed; resolved from eyes and gravity, learned by the affine fit, tested for standard, flipped, mirrored and rotated frames.
- Forced orientation: removed; `viewMatrix(for:)` receives the window scene orientation.
- Double mirror / double conversion / calibration applied twice: the mapper applies axis mapping, nominal frame, affine once; smoothing happens after conversion to points, once, in the session. Verified by `GazeMapperTests` and `GameViewModelTests.calibrationApplied`.
- Wrong viewport: viewport comes from the actual view size, stored in the profile, compared within 1 percent.
- Off-screen coordinates: clamped to a 50 percent overshoot band.
- NaN / Infinity: rejected at every stage (ray, mapper, aggregator, fit, criteria).
- ARSession retention: delegate is weak; the shared tracker's callbacks are owned by one screen at a time (`ownsGaze`), regression test added.
- MainActor / concurrency: unchanged model; the simulated oracle timer hops to the main actor explicitly.
